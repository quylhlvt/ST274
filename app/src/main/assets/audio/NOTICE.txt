Bark acoustic model

bark-embedding.tflite exposes YAMNet's 1024-dimensional pooled embedding.
YAMNet: Copyright Google LLC, Apache License 2.0 (LICENSE-2.0.txt).
https://github.com/tensorflow/models/tree/master/research/audioset/yamnet

bark-head.bin is an ST274 binary RBF head trained on acoustic events, not words.
Training sources (CC BY 4.0):
- Vocal Imitation Set v1.0, Bongjun Kim and Bryan Pardo, Northwestern University.
  https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1249724
- VocalSketch v1.0.4, Mark Cartwright and Bryan Pardo, Northwestern University.
  https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.13862
- Speech Commands v0.02 test files, Pete Warden / Google.
  https://storage.googleapis.com/download.tensorflow.org/data/speech_commands_test_set_v0.02.tar.gz

CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
CC0 desk-knock training sources on Freesound:
- testing_player: https://freesound.org/people/testing_player/sounds/243033/
- Hephaestus: https://freesound.org/people/Hephaestus/sounds/250139/
- Mrguff: https://freesound.org/people/Mrguff/sounds/369710/
- Rvgerxini: https://freesound.org/people/Rvgerxini/sounds/462317/
- Yoshicakes77: https://freesound.org/people/Yoshicakes77/sounds/645927/

Source audio has been segmented, resampled and used to fit the classifier.
No user microphone recordings were used to train this head or bundled here.
See docs/bark-detection.md and tools/audio/train_bark_head.py for validation
limits, the held-out speaker split, and export format.
